Area Guide

Brief overview of the communities where your listings are located.

Greeley, CO
The largest city in Weld County, home to University of Northern Colorado. Strong job market anchored by agriculture, healthcare, and energy sectors. Good access to I-25 and US-34. Manufactured home parks are well-established here with multiple all-ages communities. Most affordable overall area in the search with the highest concentration of listings.
Highest listing countMost affordableI-25 access
Evans, CO
Adjacent to Greeley, Evans offers affordable housing with quick access to Greeley's amenities. Home to several established manufactured home communities. Smaller-town feel with the convenience of a larger metro nearby. Generally lower lot rents than Loveland or Fort Collins.
Greeley adjacentAffordableEstablished parks
Loveland, CO
Known as the "Gateway to Rocky Mountain National Park," Loveland is a mid-sized city with a strong arts scene, Benson Sculpture Garden, and proximity to Lake Loveland. Strong demand for housing keeps prices higher here, but manufactured home communities — particularly Sylmar Village and Aspen Ridge — offer excellent value. Several 55+ parks available here.
Rocky Mtn accessMultiple 55+ parksArts community
Fort Collins, CO
Home to Colorado State University, Fort Collins consistently ranks among Colorado's most livable cities. Higher overall cost of living, but manufactured home options at Cloverleaf and Harmony offer access to this market. Cloverleaf is all-ages and pet-friendly. Lot rents are highest in the search here (~$1,295/mo at Cloverleaf).
CSU / College townHigher lot rentsAll-ages Cloverleaf
Milliken & Pierce, CO
Smaller communities east of Greeley on the plains. Milliken (Evergreen community) offers newer construction manufactured homes at affordable prices. Pierce (Highland Mobile Home Park) is a rural option further north. These areas offer some of the most affordable lot rents in the search and are worth considering if driving distance to Greeley is acceptable.

Age-Restricted Community / HOPA Disclosure

Listings identified with a "55+ Restricted" badge are located in communities that claim age qualification under the Housing for Older Persons Act of 1995 (HOPA), 42 U.S.C. §3607(b). HOPA requires that (1) at least 80% of occupied units be occupied by at least one person 55 years of age or older, and (2) the housing facility or community publish and follow policies and procedures that demonstrate its intent to be housing for persons 55 and older. A household that includes a minor child (under 18) or teenager will typically not qualify to reside in a HOPA-compliant 55+ community. Zachary Springborn and JPAR® Modern Real Estate make no representation as to the current qualification status of any park. Buyer must independently verify age-restriction rules, current occupancy ratios, and household qualification requirements directly with community management prior to making an offer. The information herein is not legal advice.

Lot Rent Disclosure

Manufactured and mobile homes in this search are located on leased land (lot rent communities). The purchase price of the home does not include ownership of the land. Monthly lot rent is an ongoing obligation separate from and in addition to any mortgage payment on the home itself. Lot rent amounts shown are as reported by listing agents in MLS remarks and have not been independently verified. Lot rent is subject to increase; most Colorado communities require 60-90 days notice of rent increases. Buyer should review the full park lease agreement before making any offer and should factor ongoing lot rent into total cost of ownership calculations.
