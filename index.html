Price vs. Space: At under $80K, the sweet spot for Pedro is the 1,200–1,280 sq ft tier — several homes there fall in the $65K–$78K range and still leave room to negotiate. Below 900 sq ft the prices drop dramatically but so does livability for a two-person household.

Lot Rent Is a Real Monthly Cost: Several parks charge $992–$1,375/month. At $1,200/mo that’s $14,400/year — more than the purchase price of some listings in this search. Always compare the all-in monthly cost (lot rent + utilities + any insurance) when evaluating options, not just the purchase price.

All-Ages vs. 55+ Landscape: Slightly more than half the search (13 of 25 listings) are in age-restricted communities. Given Pedro’s situation, the 12 all-ages listings are the practical short list. Greeley and Evans have the highest concentration of all-ages communities at this price point.

Newest Inventory to Prioritize: Five listings show 1 day on market as of this report date. These are the freshest additions to the market and should get attention first before they attract competing offers: MLS #6934 (Greeley, 1,216 sf), #6963 (Evans, 1,216 sf), #6974 (Loveland, 960 sf), #6967 (Greeley, 924 sf), and #6869 (Loveland, 896 sf — 55+ restricted).

Fort Collins Premium: Cloverleaf and Harmony Village homes in Fort Collins command slightly higher prices for equivalent square footage compared to Greeley/Evans options, reflecting overall demand in the Fort Collins market. If budget is the binding constraint, Greeley/Evans delivers the best square footage per dollar.

Communities in Pedro’s Search

Quick orientation to each area where listings are located.

Greeley & Evans

The core of Pedro’s search. Weld County’s seat (pop. ~105K) with major employers including JBS, Banner Health, and UNC. Evans borders Greeley to the south. Several all-ages communities here — Villa West, Stoneybrook, Cave Creek, Friendly Village — make this the priority cluster for Pedro’s household.

Loveland

~30 minutes southwest via US-34. Known for arts, proximity to Rocky Mountain National Park, and strong infrastructure. Most manufactured home parks here (Aspen Ridge, Sylmar, Sunset MHC) are 55+ restricted. The exception is Lago Vista (all-ages), which has two listings in this search at 960–1,200 sq ft.

Fort Collins

Colorado State University’s city, 20–25 minutes west of Greeley on US-34/I-25. Excellent transit, schools, and services. All-ages Cloverleaf community has two listings in this search including the largest home (1,280 sf, MLS #6881). Harmony Village (all-ages) also has a well-remodeled 924 sf option.

Cloverleaf all-ages — largest home in the search

55+ Age-Restricted Community Disclosures

Properties flagged as 55+ communities are subject to the Housing for Older Persons Act (HOPA), 42 U.S.C. § 3607(b). Under HOPA, qualifying communities may lawfully restrict occupancy to persons 55 years of age or older, provided that at least 80% of occupied units are occupied by at least one person 55 or older and the community maintains and follows policies demonstrating intent to be housing for older persons. Rules regarding secondary and additional occupants vary significantly by community. Many parks require that ALL residents, not only the purchasing owner, meet minimum age requirements. The minimum secondary-occupant age at parks in this search ranges from 40 to 55 years of age. A minor or teenage son would almost certainly not qualify for residency at any 55+ community in this search. Zachary Springborn is not an attorney and nothing in this report constitutes legal advice regarding HOPA compliance or any individual park's occupancy requirements. Pedro should speak directly with each park manager and consult legal counsel before assuming any household member will be permitted to reside at an age-restricted community.

HUD Certification, Chattel Titling & Financing

Manufactured homes built after June 15, 1976 should carry a HUD certification label (data plate). The presence or absence of a HUD label affects financing eligibility, insurability, and resale. Buyers should confirm the existence and validity of the HUD label for any home they intend to purchase. Homes titled as personal property (chattel) rather than real property are financed differently from site-built homes. Buyers should consult with a manufactured-home-experienced lender — such as 21st Mortgage, Triad Financial Services (TriadFS), or other specialty lenders — regarding financing options including chattel loans, FHA Title I, and conventional lending where applicable. Financing availability is subject to lender qualification requirements and is not guaranteed by Zachary Springborn or JPAR® Modern Real Estate.

Resale & Appreciation Disclosures

Manufactured homes on leased land generally do not appreciate in value at the same rate as site-built homes or manufactured homes on owned land. The ability to resell is dependent upon park approval of the subsequent buyer, continued availability of lot rent, and prevailing market conditions at time of resale. Land lease terms, potential rent increases, and park closure or conversion risk should be evaluated as part of Pedro's due diligence.
